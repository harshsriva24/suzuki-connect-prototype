Same app, three additions

The phone mirrors the public Suzuki Connect app: Live Dashboard, Location & Tracking with Geofence, Trips with Trip History, Driving Analysis and Leaderboard, Remote Operations and the Alerts feed. Nothing in that structure changes. Suzuki Connect 2.0 adds:

  1. Plan strip on Home (Safe · Plus · Care+) under the dashboard card. Tap it for Plans & Subscription, which replaces today's renewal web form.
  2. Care+ inside Vehicle Health Check on the Remote tab: the car predicts a fault, the owner books a slot at their own dealer in two taps, pick-up included.
  3. Safe-driver discount inside Driving Analysis on the Trips tab: the existing Driving Score, now with a consent switch for a discount-only insurance offer.

The line, built into the UI

Safe is locked on. No screen offers to sell SOS, crash alert or recalls. Data has a switch. The insurance score only reads driving data after opt-in and stops the moment the owner switches it off. App price equals dealer price. Every plan screen shows the same ₹ figure the dealer quotes.

Try the loop

Open Remote, book the battery slot under Vehicle Health, then switch to the Dealer and Maruti console: the job appears in the Kanpur ARENA queue and the ledger updates. Turn Plus on from the plan strip and the attach counter moves too.

Example data for one car. Prices are proposals from the team's plan. Layout, tab bar, colours and copy follow the App Store screenshots of Suzuki Connect v1.1 (2025); the app's typeface is not published, so Nunito Sans stands in.
